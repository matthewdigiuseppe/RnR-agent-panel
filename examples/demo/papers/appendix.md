# Online Appendix

## A1. Event-study estimates

Figure A1 plots year-by-year estimates relative to 2010, the last pre-treatment
year. The pre-treatment coefficients for 2006, 2007, 2008 and 2009 are
individually and jointly indistinguishable from zero (joint F-test p = 0.71).
The post-treatment coefficients are negative and grow in magnitude through 2015.

## A2. Balance and covariate trends

Table A2 reports differences in pre-treatment trends in incumbent vote share,
unemployment, and population between treated and untreated municipalities. No
difference is significant at conventional levels.

## A3. Summary statistics

Table A3 reports means and standard deviations for all variables, separately for
treated and untreated municipalities.

## A4. Alternative specifications

Table A4 reports estimates using a stacked event-study estimator robust to
heterogeneous treatment timing, and estimates dropping municipalities within 2
percentage points of the assignment threshold. The point estimate ranges from
-3.4 to -4.1 across specifications.

## A5. Subgroup analyses

Table A5 reports all fourteen heterogeneity splits we examined, with unadjusted
p-values. We do not report multiplicity-adjusted p-values.
